Edge/error cases:
- convert a lead that is not qualified -> 409; converting twice -> 409
- link a quote of another org -> 409; link to a closed opportunity -> 409
- move to lost without lost_reason -> 409; move a won opportunity back to an open stage clears closed_at
- deactivating the only won or lost stage -> 409
- forecast with no opportunities -> zero buckets; opportunity with no close date -> 'no date' bucket; past date -> 'overdue'
- weighted rounding: 333 cents at 50% -> 167
- agreement with no end_date -> no reminders; end_date in the past -> none; end in 100 days with {90,30} -> none; 89 days -> the 90 reminder and the renewal opportunity; 29 days -> the 30 reminder only, no second opportunity
- renewal_email set but no mailbox -> reminder created, no email
- renewal opportunity won or lost -> its open reminders marked done
- dismissed reminder is not recreated
