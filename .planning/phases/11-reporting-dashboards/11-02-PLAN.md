<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 2: Report schedules and worker emails</name>
  <files>backend/app/report_schedules.py, backend/app/worker.py, backend/tests/test_report_schedules.py</files>
  <action>
report_schedules.py: create/update/list schedules and list runs (audit-recorded writes). Validation: recipient_user_ids must be existing active users who hold report:read (permissions MATRIX for their role) at save time, else Conflict naming the user; weekly needs weekday, monthly needs day_of_month (1-28). due_period(schedule, today): weekly -> the previous Monday..Sunday when today.weekday() >= schedule.weekday in the week after that period, i.e. period = (monday_of_this_week - 7 days, monday_of_this_week - 1 day) and due once today.weekday() >= weekday; monthly -> the previous calendar month, due once today.day >= day_of_month. Return None when not yet due. run_due(ctx, today): for each active schedule with a due period and no run row for (schedule_id, period_start): re-check recipients at send time (active users holding report:read; drop the rest); none left -> insert run status 'skipped' with detail; else build (filename, csv) with report_metrics.csv_for(ctx with Scope.all(), report, period_start, period_end) (aging ignores the period: as of today) and queue one outbound EmailMessage to those users' email addresses with the CSV as an OutboundAttachment, subject f'{schedule.name}: {period_start} to {period_end}', following exactly how notices.send_notice creates the outbound message and attachment rows; insert run 'sent' with recipients and email_message_id. Each schedule runs in its own savepoint (db.begin_nested()); an exception records run 'failed' with the error text and does not stop the others. The unique (schedule_id, period_start) makes repeats no-ops. worker.py: add report_jobs() following billing_jobs (new session, set_org_scope 'all', Ctx with Scope.all(), commit, log counts, log.exception on failure) and call it from main with the other jobs. test_report_schedules.py: due_period for weekly and monthly around boundaries; run_due sends once per period (second call creates nothing); recipients who lost report:read are dropped and a schedule with none left is 'skipped'; one failing schedule does not block another; the queued email has the CSV attachment and only staff addresses; saving a schedule with a tech recipient is refused.
